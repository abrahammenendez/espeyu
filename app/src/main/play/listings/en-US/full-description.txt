A full-screen mirror that shows the true view by default, with freeze-frame, zoom, brightness control, and an on-screen ring light.

Your phone's front camera shows you flipped, like a mirror. Nobody sees you that way. Espeyu lets you see yourself as others do, and the mirrored view is still there when you want it.

Features:

* Use the front or back camera, full screen.
* Switch between the true view and the mirrored view.
* Freeze the picture to look at it closely without holding still.
* Pinch to zoom.
* Set the screen brightness without changing your phone's setting.
* Light your face with a ring light around the edge of the screen, from daylight to warm.
* Use it upright or sideways.

Espeyu only asks for camera access. It has no way to connect to the internet, so nothing you do in it ever leaves your phone.

No photos, no video, no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics.

Free and open source: https://github.com/abrahammenendez/espeyu
