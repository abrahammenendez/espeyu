Open the app and you see yourself, full screen, straight away.

Every phone shows you a mirrored front camera preview, and no setting turns
that off: the "mirror front camera" option on Android and iOS only changes how
a saved photo is written, never the live view. Espeyu shows you the true view
by default, the way other people see you, and lets you flip back to a
mirrored, bathroom-mirror view whenever you want.

Features:

* Full-screen viewfinder on the front or back camera
* True view and mirrored view, switched live
* Freeze the frame to hold a look still, which also releases the camera
* Pinch to zoom
* Screen brightness control that leaves the system setting alone
* An on-screen ring light that frames the mirror, with a warmth slider
* Portrait and landscape

Espeyu takes no photos, records no video, writes nothing to disk except your
own preferences, and holds no accounts. It asks for the camera permission and
nothing else. There is no INTERNET permission in the manifest, so it cannot
phone home even if it wanted to, and there are no ads, no analytics and no
crash reporting.
