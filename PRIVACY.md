# Privacy policy for Espeyu

Effective 11 September 2026.

Espeyu is a mirror app. It shows you a live camera preview.

## What Espeyu collects

Nothing. No personal data, no usage data, no identifiers, no analytics, no
crash reports.

The app has no `INTERNET` permission, so it has no way to send anything
anywhere. `CAMERA` is the only permission it asks for.

## The camera

Camera frames are drawn on the screen and discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved
to your photos, or transmitted. Freezing releases the camera and keeps a single
frame in memory, never on disk, until you unfreeze the view, switch camera, or
close the app.

## What is stored on your device

Six preferences: which camera you were using, whether you were in the true or
the mirrored view, whether the screen brightness override was on and at what
level, and whether the ring light was on and at what warmth.

They are kept in the app's private storage. They are excluded from cloud backup
and from device transfer, so they never leave the phone, and uninstalling the
app deletes them.

## Children

Espeyu is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published in this file, and its history is
public in the repository.
